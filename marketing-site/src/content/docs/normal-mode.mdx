# Normal Mode

Normal Mode is the everyday experience in FormMirror. It’s fast, forgiving, and requires only a screenshot.

## Bring in your screenshot

- **Drag & drop:** Drop a PNG or JPG onto the window.
- **Paste:** Copy from any capture tool and hit `⌘/Ctrl + V`.
- **File picker:** Click **Upload screenshot** and browse.

## Review panel

FormMirror groups matches by confidence:

- **Auto-fill:** High confidence matches fill instantly.
- **Review:** Mid confidence matches wait for your confirmation.
- **Manual:** Low confidence matches display options; click to remap.

Use the inline controls to reassign labels, tweak case sensitivity, or skip fields entirely. Passwords and file inputs are always skipped by default.

## Smart field handling

- Dates and times respect locale formatting and fall back to native pickers.
- Select inputs fire proper `change` events.
- Contenteditable regions send synthetic typing events.

## Preprocessing toggles

Tough screenshots? Toggle optional preprocessing:

- **Contrast boost**: Clarify low-contrast scans.
- **Noise reduction**: Smooth grainy faxes.
- **Sharpen**: Crisp edges for OCR clarity.

Each change re-runs OCR locally, so experimentation never leaves your machine.
